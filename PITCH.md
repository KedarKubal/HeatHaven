# HeatHaven — 2-minute pitch script

**Track:** Resilient Cities & Buildings  
**Target:** −25% building energy-use intensity by 2035 (COP31 Action Agenda)

---

**0:00–0:25 — Problem**

Extreme heat is already hitting Australian and Pacific neighbourhoods. Dark roofs, thin shade, and leaky buildings drive cooling demand up — and leave people at risk when the grid and the mercury both spike. COP31 asks us to cut building-sector energy intensity by at least 25% by 2035. Most councils still plan interventions in spreadsheets, not as a clear path to that number.

**0:25–0:45 — User & solution**

HeatHaven is for city planners and community organisers. Pick a neighbourhood — Melbourne, Auckland, Suva, or Port Vila — see heat hotspots, toggle cool roofs, shade, insulation, and cooling hubs, and instantly see energy intensity, heat risk, and people protected against the 2035 target.

**0:45–1:25 — Live demo**

1. Open HeatHaven.  
2. Scroll to the planner; select **Suva / Raiwaqa**.  
3. Show the heat map hotspots.  
4. Toggle cool roofs + cooling hubs; point at the progress bar toward −25%.  
5. Add shade canopy; show people protected rising.  
6. Copy the 90-day action brief.

**1:25–1:50 — Impact path**

We are not claiming a finished product. The next step is a 90-day pilot: twenty buildings, three outdoor sensors, one cooling hub — measure kWh/m² and heat exposure before and after. If the gap to −25% closes, scale the winning mix across the city.

**1:50–2:00 — Close**

HeatHaven turns COP31 ambition into a neighbourhood plan you can test this season. Cool the place people live — and make the 2035 building target visible on the ground.
